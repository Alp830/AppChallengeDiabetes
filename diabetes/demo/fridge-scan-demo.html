Fridge Scan

A stand-alone copy of the Wellness Companion fridge scanner. Take or upload up to 3 photos of a fridge, pantry or grocery bag; the foods appear as they are found. Items the scan isn't sure of are marked Maybe with alternatives, and any item can be removed with ✕. Nothing is stored: this page only talks to Google's Gemini API.

1. Your Gemini API key

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. The scan uses gemini-4-argon, which needs billing on the key; a scan costs about $0.03 (small photo) to $0.10 (large photo). The key is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com.

2. Your photos

Tip: one photo of the shelves and one close-up of the door bins works well. Photos of 3072 px or more are read as 4 overlapping tiles for small labels.

3. What's in your fridge

How this scan works
  • The prompt works zone by zone with a count first and never leaves an item out. Every item has a confidence (sure / likely / guess), up to two alternatives and a box. Medicine, supplements and skincare are recognised and skipped.
  • Large photos keep their original pixels: they are cut into 2×2 overlapping tiles that are read in parallel, then merged by box position so items in the overlap are not counted twice.
  • Answers stream in, so the first foods appear within seconds.
  • A sharpness and exposure check warns about blurry or back-lit photos, since no model can read detail that isn't in the photo.
